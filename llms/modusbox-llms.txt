# PortX

> PortX is an AI-powered iPaaS (integration platform as a service) purpose-built for banks, credit unions, and fintechs. The PortX Platform unifies core, fintech, and enterprise integration (Integration Manager), a governed real-time data lakehouse (Data Manager), and modern payments orchestration including ACH, wires, RTP, and FedNow (Payment Manager), with PiXi AI as the embedded intelligence layer. ORCA (Open Reusable Core API) provides a universal, standards-based connection to major banking cores so financial institutions can modernize without ripping and replacing the systems that work.

PortX serves community banks, credit unions, regional banks, and fintech partners. The company is SOC 2 Type II certified, ISO/IEC 27001 certified, and aligns to FAPI, FDX, ISO 20022, CUFX, and PCI standards. Contact: hello@portx.io / 1.833.667.6789.

## Products

- [Integration Manager](https://portx.io/products/integration-manager): An API-led iPaaS purpose-built for banks and credit unions; standardizes connectivity to cores, fintechs, and enterprise apps using reusable APIs and the Connect Marketplace.
- [Data Manager](https://portx.io/products/data-manager): A real-time, governed data lakehouse that captures every transaction and fintech connection into a single source of truth and powers Customer 360, powered by PiXi AI.
- [Payment Manager](https://portx.io/products/payment-manager): Unified payments orchestration across ACH, wires, RTP, and FedNow with automated workflows and real-time visibility.
- [PiXi AI](https://portx.io/pixi-ai): The AI foundation of the PortX Platform — natural-language insights, AI-powered data mapping, automation, and compliance-first design embedded across integration, data, and payments.
- [ORCA (Open Reusable Core API) one-pager](https://portx.io/print/orca-onepager): Universal, portable, standards-based API specification (JSON/REST, aligned with ISO 20022, CUFX, FDX) for connecting to all major banking cores.
